Youth Innovation

SBSC Supports Youth Innovation for Sustainable Agri-Aqua

Siargao's Bounty Seafoods Corporation (SBSC), through CEO Mr. Philip V. Senora II, shared industry insights with students and faculty from State Universities and Colleges across Eastern Visayas during LIYAB SparkLab 2025, hosted by Visayas State University.

The engagement focused on industry challenges, innovation opportunities, and the role of young innovators in developing practical and sustainable solutions for the agri-aqua sector.

SBSC is grateful to be part of this meaningful exchange that supports innovation, youth development, and sustainable growth in the marine and aquaculture industries.

Photo credits: Visayas State University Facebook Page

International Expo · South Korea

BISFE 2025 — Busan, South Korea

Siargao's Bounty Seafoods Corporation proudly represented the Philippines at the Busan International Seafood and Fisheries Expo (BISFE) 2025, held at BEXCO Hall, Busan, South Korea from November 5–7, 2025.

This meaningful participation was made possible through the invaluable support of the Department of Agriculture, the Philippine Agriculture Office in Seoul, and DA-AMAS. Siargao's Bounty Seafoods expresses its heartfelt gratitude to the government for continuously empowering Filipino seafood exporters and strengthening their global market presence.

International Expo · South Korea

Siargao's Bounty Seafoods Corp. Joins BISFE 2024 for the Third Consecutive Year

For the third year in a row, Siargao's Bounty Seafoods Corp. (SBSC) proudly participated in the Busan International Seafood & Fisheries Expo (BISFE), held from November 6-8, 2024, at the Busan Exhibition and Convention Center (BEXCO) in South Korea. Known as one of Asia's largest and most influential seafood trade fairs, BISFE brings together global players in the seafood industry, presenting an array of premium seafood products, trends, and innovations.

Company Event · 2025

September 27–29, 2025 · Team Building

SBSC Team Building 2025 — Dalaguete, Cebu

Siargao's Bounty Seafoods Corp. (SBSC) successfully held its 2025 Team Building Activity last September 27–29, 2025 at Cocina Calza Shore, Dalaguete, Cebu. The two-day event was actively participated in by employees from the administrative office, department heads, and team leaders.

The activity was designed to foster unity, enhance collaboration, and strengthen organizational culture. Participants were divided into four groups, each tasked to create a group name and cheer—building team spirit and camaraderie from the start.
