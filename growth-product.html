Award · Product Innovation

We Did It! Award-Winning Innovation!

Siargao's Bounty Seafoods Corporation proudly takes home the "MOST INNOVATIVE PRODUCT AWARD" at the Sinulog Trade Fair 2026, organized by DTI–Cebu! The awarding ceremony capped off the successful trade fair held from January 14–21, 2026 at SM City Cebu Atrium.

This achievement celebrates our passion for innovation, sustainability, and excellence. At Siargao's Bounty, we believe that every part of the sea's bounty matters—turning seafood into products that are not only delicious and nutritious, but also responsibly and creatively made.

Trade Fair · 2026

Siargao's Bounty Seafoods is at the Sinulog Fiesta Fair 2026!

Siargao's Bounty Seafoods Corp. proudly took part in the Sinulog Fiesta Fair 2026, held at the SM City Cebu Atrium from January 14–21, 2026. The trade fair was organized by DTI–Cebu, showcasing outstanding local products and enterprises.

Product Launch · November 28, 2024

SBSC Launches Its Seafood Value-Added Products

Siargao's Bounty Seafoods Corp. (SBSC) proudly unveiled its newest line of seafood value-added products under the brand name YUMI on November 28, 2024. Inspired by the charm and innocence of childhood, YUMI reflects the brand's playful and endearing nature, offering products that are tender in texture and delightfully sweet in appeal.

This launch aligns with its vision to expand offerings to local markets alongside its well-established export ventures. This milestone reflects SBSC's unwavering commitment to producing high-quality, innovative seafood products that are not only accessible to Filipino consumers but also continue to uphold the company's strong reputation in international markets.
