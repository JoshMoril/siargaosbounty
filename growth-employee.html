MBA Achievement

Congratulations, Mr. Rickfel M. Cayon, MBA! 🎓

We are proud to celebrate the remarkable achievement of our very own Mr. Rickfel M. Cayon, MBA, who has successfully earned his Master of Business Administration (MBA) from the University of the Visayas! 🎓

This milestone is a reflection of Mr. Rickfel's dedication, perseverance, discipline, and commitment to continuous learning and professional growth. Balancing the demands of work and graduate studies takes determination. And today, we celebrate the result of that hard work.

Bachelor's Degree

OJT Program

SBSC Supports Future Industry Leaders Through OJT Program

Siargao's Bounty Seafoods Corporation provided On-the-Job Training (OJT) opportunities to students from various schools and universities, giving them hands-on experience and practical knowledge in seafood manufacturing and processing.

Through the program, SBSC supported students in developing industry-relevant skills and preparing for their future careers.

HR · Seminar

Investing in Our People and Shifting With the Times

Our team recently attended the AVEGA Brew event in Cebu last September 23!

It was an insightful session led by Atty. Regal Oliva on "The Workplace Has Changed: What HR Leaders Need to Know About People, Policy, and the Law." We gained valuable updates on modern labor trends, policy navigation, and people management strategies to ensure our organization remains a supportive and compliant environment for everyone.
