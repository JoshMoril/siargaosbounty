Charity · Faith

A Heartfelt Thanksgiving to Mama Mary

Siargao's Bounty Seafoods Corporation joins the community in celebrating the birthday of our beloved Mama Mary.

Outreach · Feeding Program

Giving Back Through Blessings

In celebration of the birth month of the pillars of Siargao's Bounty Seafoods Corporation — Mr. Philip Señora II and Mrs. Liza Señora — together with the family matriarch, Madam Anacleta Señora, who all celebrated their birthdays this April, the company conducted a heartfelt outreach program for the residents of various puroks in Linao, Minglanilla, Cebu.

A total of 1,000 children were blessed through a feeding initiative, where the company shared its very own Abalone Congee. Packed with essential nutrients such as protein, iron, zinc, Omega-3 fatty acids, Vitamin B12, and iodine, this meal supports children's growth, brain development, and overall health.

More than just a feeding activity, this initiative is a way of giving back for the many blessings the Señora family has received. It reflects their commitment to share these blessings with those in need. Over the years, this has become both a family and corporate tradition anchored in the mission of helping and uplifting fellow Filipinos.

Together, we continue to nourish lives, strengthen communities, and spread kindness. 💛

Faith · Outreach

112th Birth Anniversary of Venerable Archbishop Teofilo Camomot

In celebration of the 112th Birth Anniversary of Venerable Archbishop Teofilo Camomot, Siargao's Bounty Seafoods Corporation shared its blessings by distributing food packs to the devotees who attended the birthday Mass last March 3, 2026, at the Teofilo Camomot Shrine in Villadolid, Carcar City.

Through the collective effort and volunteerism of its active employees, the company prepared and distributed a total of 700 food packs after the Mass. This initiative reflects the company's continued commitment to community service and faith-driven outreach.

As an active benefactor of the shrine, Siargao's Bounty Seafoods Corporation remains dedicated to supporting meaningful causes and strengthening its partnership with the local community.

Sinulog · Community Service

2,000 Food Packs for Sinulog Volunteers

SBSC delivered 2,000 food packs for the Sinulog Foot Procession volunteers on the vísperas of the Feast of Señor Sto. Niño de Cebu.

SBSC employees tirelessly prepared food packs for the foot procession volunteers, which included the well-loved Octopus Bola-bola and Taco Balls. This meaningful activity has long been part of the company's tradition.

This initiative is humbly offered as a thanksgiving for all the blessings and guidance the company has received throughout the year.

Sinulog Fiesta · January 15, 2026

Penitential Walk with Mary

As part of the Sinulog Fiesta, the Penitential Walk with Mary gathered thousands of devotees in a solemn procession from Fuente Osmeña to the Basilica Minore del Sto. Niño.

In line with its commitment and core values, Siargao's Bounty Seafoods Corporation took part in this meaningful event by preparing 500 meals for the dedicated volunteers. The meals were lovingly prepared by the company's employees, reflecting the organization's deep belief in faith, service, and compassion for the community.

This act of sharing embodies the company's values — serving not only through business excellence, but through faith-driven service.

December · Outreach

Blessings for the Basilica del Santo Niño

Another day in December marked another meaningful opportunity for Siargao's Bounty Seafoods Corporation (SBSC) to share its blessings. This time, 77 maintenance and security personnel of the Basilica del Santo Niño de Cebu were gifted 5-kilo sacks of rice along with the company's own octopus trimmings.

Healthcare Heroes

Appreciation for VSMMC Personnel

Siargao's Bounty Seafoods Corporation (SBSC) expressed its heartfelt appreciation for those who tirelessly care for people in times of illness. As a simple gesture of gratitude, SBSC distributed 5-kilo sacks of rice to 55 security personnel, maintenance staff, and Malasakit personnel who continue to serve patients every day at Vicente Sotto Memorial Medical Center (VSMMC).

Holiday Blessings

Sharing With Our Neighboring Communities

Siargao's Bounty Seafoods Corporation extended its holiday blessings by distributing 65 packs of 5-kilo rice with octopus trimmings to nearby communities surrounding the company's location. The beneficiaries included residents from Sitio Atyang, Kasambagan, and families living at the back of the SBSC plant. The distribution was held earlier this morning at the SBSC plant, bringing joy and support to our neighboring communities.

Christmas Giveaway

400 Loaves of Bread for Minglanilla

Siargao's Bounty Seafoods Corporation shared the spirit of giving through an early Christmas giveaway, distributing 400 loaves of bread to various areas and barangays of Minglanilla.

Disaster Relief

SBSC Continues Its Compassion and Support to Typhoon Tino Survivors

The distribution was led by our Executive Secretary, Ms. Nika Genobiagon, together with committed staff from the Compliance Department and Logistics Team. The children of SBSC management, Ma.
