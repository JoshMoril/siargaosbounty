Sustainability

Our Commitment

Responsibly Sourced, Sustainably Caught

Siargao's Bounty Seafoods Corporation (SBSC) adheres to manufacturing safe and high-quality fish and fishery products that are sustainably and legally caught in Philippine waters.

The company assures that each fisherman abides by the legal and statutory standards of catching marine products — to be produced with competence and best quality, and to be readily available at a cost competitive in the market.

The company is active in developing projects which could help protect and enhance the existing supply of marine products in Philippine waters through proper information dissemination and adequate training for the fishermen and the company workforce.

Seafoods served fresh from the sea. From the heart of Siargao since 2016.
